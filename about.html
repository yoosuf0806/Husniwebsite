About Me

Architect. Designer.
Problem Solver.

I'm Husni — London-based architect and project manager with a passion for creating homes that genuinely improve the way people live.

My story

I grew up fascinated by the way buildings shape experience — how a well-considered room can make you feel calm, energised, or at home in a way you can't quite articulate. That curiosity led me to study Architecture at undergraduate level, and eventually to specialise in Project Management at the University of Westminster.

Over the past four years I've worked on a range of projects across Greater London and beyond — from full residential renovations to institutional extensions and major civic infrastructure. Each project has deepened my belief that the best architecture is invisible: it simply works, looks beautiful, and makes life better.

I work with homeowners who care about quality, who want their space to reflect who they are, and who want a single trusted professional managing the entire process — from first sketch to final snag.

How I work

Every project begins with listening. Before I pick up a pen or open Revit, I want to understand how you actually use your home. Architecture that doesn't respond to those realities is just decoration.

I use BIM software (Revit + 3D visualisation) throughout the process so you can see exactly what you're getting before anything is built. I keep my practice deliberately small — I take a limited number of projects at a time so every client receives my full attention.
