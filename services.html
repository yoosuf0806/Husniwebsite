What I Offer

Services designed
for real homes.

Every service is designed around how you actually live — not a checklist, but a considered approach to creating spaces that work beautifully.

01
Home Renovation
Residential

Whether you're reconfiguring an awkward layout, extending into the garden or completely remodelling your home, I manage the entire process — from design through planning to construction oversight. Every decision is made with your budget and lifestyle in mind.

What's included

  • Initial consultation and feasibility study
  • Concept design and space planning
  • Planning application management
  • Technical drawings and specifications
  • Contractor tender and selection
  • Construction monitoring
02
New Build Design
Residential

Designing a new home from scratch is a rare privilege — and I treat it as such. The design process begins with understanding the site, the planning context and how you want to live. What follows is a rigorous and creative journey from first sketch to build-ready drawings.

What's included

  • Site analysis and planning research
  • Concept and design development
  • Planning and building regulations
  • Structural and services coordination
  • Full construction document set
03
3D BIM Visualisation
Technology

Built in Revit and rendered to photorealistic quality, BIM models let you experience your home before a single brick is laid. Walk through every room, explore material choices and lighting conditions — and make confident decisions without expensive on-site changes.

What's included

  • Full Revit BIM model
  • Photorealistic rendered stills
  • Walkthrough animation (on request)
  • Material and finish schedules
04
Space Planning
Interior

Sometimes the issue isn't decoration — it's the layout. I specialise in finding the hidden potential in existing floor plans: removing the right wall, repositioning a staircase, opening up a kitchen. Small interventions that transform how a home feels.

What's included

  • Measured survey and existing drawings
  • Options analysis and feasibility
  • Proposed layout and section drawings
  • Furniture and circulation planning
05
Interior Design
Interior

From material palettes to bespoke joinery details, I bring the same rigour to interior design as to architecture. The goal is always coherence — spaces that feel considered and complete, where every element belongs.

What's included

  • Interior concept and mood boards
  • Material and finish specification
  • Furniture selection and layout
  • Lighting design and specification
  • Joinery and bespoke element design
06
Planning & Approvals
Compliance

Navigating the planning system can be daunting. I manage the entire process — from pre-application discussions with the local authority through to decision. I know what planners respond to and how to present proposals persuasively.

What's included

  • Pre-application advice and strategy
  • Planning drawings and design statements
  • Application submission and management
  • Neighbour consultation support
  • Appeal support if required
